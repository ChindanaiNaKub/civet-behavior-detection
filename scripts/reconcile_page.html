Reconcile the two gold passes

Two people labelled these clips independently. Here you line the passes up with the video and decide one final set of intervals per clip, together, using the annotation guide. The page records what you choose and never decides for you. A clip's final intervals do not touch data/ until you run python scripts/reconcile.py --apply.

Judge by the rules, not by who labelled it. Resting needs 5 s of continuous stillness (counted from its start); Eating needs visible food-directed action; use Uncertain with a reason when the video does not show enough. Where one behavior was split into back-to-back pieces, decide whether it is really one interval: a window needs a single interval to cover it.
