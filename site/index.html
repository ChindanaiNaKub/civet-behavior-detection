03YOLO detection

0.537mAP50 on validation
59%held-out frames where the animal is found

No bounding box was drawn by hand. A per-clip background model proposes the boxes, 116 of 408 were audited visually and 113 contained the animal, then YOLO trains on them. The detector does not carry across recording days, so the demo falls back to the per-clip model and the box colour on the video says which one fired.

04Silhouette features

0.422macro F1, locked test
0.190majority-guess baseline

Keypoints are replaced by four silhouette features: body angle, head angle, image speed and limb extent. The gain over the majority guess is in the rare classes, not in getting most windows right.

The four features

05What this does not prove

The test split holds 15 windows, so a single window moves macro F1 by several points. The validation split is perfectly separated, which says the split is easy, not that the classifier generalizes.

Task 2's detector fires on 59 percent of held-out frames and about half of those boxes sit on the animal.

Task 3's features are not keypoints. Body angle and head angle are two axes of one blob, and 7 of the 15 test windows have no silhouette at all, so their rows are the training median rather than measured geometry.
