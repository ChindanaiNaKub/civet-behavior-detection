# Behavior dataset

This directory holds the tracked source clips and the source-clip ledger and CSV templates for the reviewed behavior-interval dataset. The 22 clips are committed under [`raw/`](raw/) exactly as delivered; the source archive is not copied here.

## Files

- [`source-clips.csv`](source-clips.csv) lists the 22 audited source clips. `folder_label_weak` is the original folder name, not a verified behavior. `capture_datetime_overlay` and `camera_id_overlay` are transcribed from the sampled on-screen overlay; timezone, room, and recording session remain unknown.
- [`annotations.csv`](annotations.csv) is the interval-level annotation table. `scripts/review.py` writes it: one row per annotator and interval, and re-saving a clip replaces that annotator's rows for it rather than adding a second copy. `behavior` accepts Eating, Locomotion, Resting, Climbing, Transition, or Uncertain; Transition and Uncertain are recorded as separate interval labels and are not model classes. Annotators must work independently on the shared sample before discussing it. Use the boundary rules in [`../docs/ANNOTATION-GUIDE.md`](../docs/ANNOTATION-GUIDE.md).
- [`agreement.csv`](agreement.csv) records shared-sample review, disagreements, and their resolution. `scripts/agreement.py` writes the measured columns from two annotation files and leaves `resolved_labels`, `resolution_reason`, `reviewed_by` and `reviewed_at` for the two annotators to fill. The second annotator's rows go in `annotations-second.csv`.
- [`gold-clips.txt`](gold-clips.txt) lists the eight gold clips, one id per line. They are the test split. Two different people label them blind (see the README for the commands), the first into `annotations.csv` and the second into `annotations-second.csv`.
- [`splits.csv`](splits.csv) is the source-clip split ledger, generated by `scripts/split.py`. Every clip is assigned to train, validation or test, and `scripts/split.py` refuses an assignment that leaves a model class with no verified window in a split. The split is by whole clip rather than by recording group, because camera and class are confounded in this footage ([ADR 0005](../docs/adr/0005-split-by-clip-not-by-recording-group.md)); the clips were chosen on the verified windows in [ADR 0008](../docs/adr/0008-resplit-on-verified-windows-gold-is-test.md). `grouping_key` records the `(camera, date)` group and `assignment_reason` records whether that group appears in more than one split. `test_locked_by` and `test_locked_at` are left empty for a person to fill when they accept the assignment.

## Dataset state

`data/annotations.csv` holds 62 intervals covering all 22 clips from one annotator, who reviewed 14 of them against the machine proposal and labeled the eight gold clips blind. They are a first pass, not a settled dataset: all eight gold clips are now labeled blind by the first annotator, but the gold set has no second annotator yet, so `agreement.csv` is empty (ADR 0008). The test lock in `splits.csv` is empty. No Climbing clip was supplied, the supplied archive is the whole dataset, and the two candidate Climbing intervals were reviewed and are not climbing, so Climbing is out of scope as a model class ([ADR 0007](../docs/adr/0007-drop-climbing-from-phase-2-scope.md)). Do not treat an empty field as a negative label or treat a folder name as ground truth.

The source ledger has 11 clips from Camera 02 and 11 from Camera 03, with no recording-session IDs, and the two cameras do not carry the same behavior mix: 597 of the 659 seconds of Resting are Camera 03 on 2026-05-19, and every Locomotion clip is on 2026-05-18. That is why `scripts/split.py` splits by clip and records the group straddle on every row instead of holding a camera out.

Lighting, visibility, camera view, and occlusion vary by interval. `scripts/lighting.py` fills lighting from whether the sampled frames carry colour, and visibility and occlusion only from the annotator's own `uncertain_reason`; everything else stays `unknown`. Record other conditions only when observed; use `unknown` when evidence is unavailable. Do not infer room, camera, or session from filenames or folder names.
